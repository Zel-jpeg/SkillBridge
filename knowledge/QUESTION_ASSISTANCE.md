# Question assistance and generated evidence text

Suggestions are advisory. Staff may accept, dismiss or manually replace a proposed
category. Both category and tag suggestion routes require an authenticated
instructor or admin. They never save question text, taxonomy, scores or placements.
Malformed text and requirement objects return field errors, rather than crashes.
Position tag requirements use the same known-category and finite 0–100 validation
as the catalog editor.

Category assistance retains its keyword/TF-IDF mechanism and 0.05 similarity
threshold. Exact category mentions respect word boundaries. Multiple explicit
category names and equal best similarities produce no suggestion. Unfamiliar
questions, no categories, empty vocabulary and model errors likewise abstain;
staff can continue manual classification. These checks establish behavior on
examples, not an accuracy claim. Close unequal scores are still heuristic advice.

Question categories are a shared taxonomy. `created_by` attributes a category;
it does not restrict another instructor from classifying their own question with
it. Question/batch ownership remains enforced. Category names must be text of at
most 100 characters. Duplicate names differing only in case are ambiguous and
require administrator resolution before a question references the name. Draft
questions may be unclassified; publication requires a category.

The browser discards a pending suggestion when its question text, manual category
or dismissal changes, or the question is removed/unmounted. A delayed response
cannot revive an older suggestion. Failure preserves text and manual selection.

Generated profiles identify category scores and contextual vocabulary. Vocabulary
terms were not separately assessed; they are not individual mastery claims. All
ties at the two leading score levels are retained in the narrative. All-zero
results have no asserted orientation and explicitly state that no assessed category
scored above zero. Version `neutral-evidence-v3` identifies newly generated text.
Existing saved profiles/history are not migrated or regenerated by this change.
Normal explicit recalculation and new submissions use the updated generator;
ranking weights remain unchanged. Position descriptions continue to use saved
requirements, percentages and role vocabulary.

## Local synthetic verification, 18 October 2026

`api.test_question_assistance` covers clear SQL, ambiguous/tied/unfamiliar questions,
no taxonomy, all-zero/tied evidence, role gates, malformed requests, shared manual
classification, duplicate-name rejection and foreign-question denial. Initial
eight tests recorded ten failed subcases and four errors; after fixes the focused
51-test suite passed. The expanded final PostgreSQL suite is recorded in the
continuous checklist. An intermediate run failed only because a new test referenced
a variable outside its method; that test was corrected and rerun.

`tests/question_assistance_playwright.py` reproduced an old response reviving a
suggestion after the text was shortened. After the fix all three browser cases
passed: ignored obsolete response; explicit accept then manual correction; service
failure retaining classification and text. APIs were synthetic and external
requests were blocked. Screenshots/results are under
`skillbridge-frontend/tests/artifacts/question-assistance/`.
