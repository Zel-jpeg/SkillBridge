## Generation prompt brief

### Overview

Create a high-fidelity desktop redesign of the SkillBridge student detail modal, using the five supplied screenshots as information-architecture references. Show Overview selected. Use white and pale gray surfaces, forest green accents, near-black headings, slate secondary text and amber only for unplaced status. Keep a prominent identity header, close control, read-only badge and all five tabs. Use the synthetic profile Alex Rivera with an initials avatar, example.edu email and OJT Batch 2026. Show required completion as 1 of 1 and 100%, clearly separate from scores and placement approval. Arrange profile/enrollment and OJT placement side by side, with one assessment snapshot below. Preserve finalized profile, unlocked recommendation access and unplaced status. Provide clear internal navigation, restrained borders, readable type and balanced whitespace. Do not invent approval, editing, exports or an overall score.

### Assessment results

Match the generated Overview shell and visual language, selecting Assessments. Show one submitted Test Assessment with Required, Included in competency and Current attempt metadata. Use a two-column body: left, an aligned category score table with proportional green bars and exact raw/max and percentage values; right, a highest-scoring-area callout and compact category-based development guidance. Preserve Web Development 3/3 and 100%, Database Management 2/3 and 66.67%, Networking 1/2 and 50%, Programming 1/2 and 50%. Show no integrity flag, zero violations and retake not approved, with retake management unavailable in this read-only view. Distinguish individual results from combined competency and archived attempts. Avoid invented grading bands, overall grades, independent skill claims or placement eligibility.
