## Sample data and meaning

The three screens use illustrative totals: 18 students awaiting placement, 12 approved placements, 20 seats not yet approved, and 5 students awaiting allocation. The five sample companies total 32 seats: 12 approved, 13 provisionally suggested and 7 available for suggestions. Suggested seats remain part of seats not yet approved; they are not approved placements. The scope labels distinguish student counts from shared capacity. Student names/IDs and history rows are synthetic.

These concepts illustrate proposed presentation, including View details actions; they do not establish implemented workflow behavior. A future implementation must retain backend scope, deterministic suggestion rules, capacity checks, approval/rejection/removal history, responsive/accessibility contracts and explicit empty states. ImageGen shapes and bars are visual approximations; implemented capacity segments must use exact numeric proportions.
